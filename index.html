FAQ

Do I need $150 for the demo?

No. The demo is free and uses only virtual orders.

Does the demo actually send USDT or money?

No. Nothing is broadcast or paid. The buttons simulate the live workflow only.

How is the 2.5% demo commission calculated?

The demo uses: order amount × 2.5%. For example, a $200 virtual order shows $5.00. The actual live commission must follow the published LP terms.

Is 2.5% guaranteed?

No. The promotional rate should be presented only when active and subject to the exact eligibility and transaction rules published by ScanSetU.
