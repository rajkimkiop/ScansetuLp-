How the partner flow works

The experience is built around three common order types.

01

Receive / Buy

A buy order arrives with an order ID and amount. You verify the incoming payment, then slide to confirm once it is actually received.

02

Send

A send order shows the order ID and the required QR or UPI ID. Follow the displayed payment instruction and slide to confirm after completion.

03

Scan & Pay

A merchant QR appears with the order details. After the scan and payment confirmation, the status updates and you complete the order.

Partner FAQ

How much liquidity do I need?

The current recruitment offer starts from $150, subject to the live partner terms and eligibility requirements.

How does commission work?

The promotional example on this page uses 2.5% of the eligible order amount. Actual commission is calculated according to the current partner terms and successful settlement conditions.

Does the experience move real money?

No. The interactive experience is a safe preview. It does not connect to a wallet, send USDT, or process a bank/UPI payment.

What should I check before confirming a real order?

Verify the actual payment/order status, order ID, amount, payment method and the exact instructions displayed by the live platform. Never confirm an order based only on a screenshot or notification.

Will ScanSetU ask for my seed phrase?

No. ScanSetU's public security information says it does not ask for seed phrases, private keys or wallet login details.
